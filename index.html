Appearance

Palette
The first color is how the timer looks while it waits, the second is how it looks while it runs. The sidebar, glow and buttons all follow it.
Brightness
Pause mode always turns the app light, whatever you pick here.
Animations
Reduced turns off looping motion (sidebar waves, aurora, ripples) to save battery and keep things quiet.

Sidebar

Wave effect
A lattice of squares that swells like water: calm while you wait, quicker and warmer while the timer runs, frosty while paused. Desktop only.
Spill onto the page
Let the squares dissolve past the sidebar's edge and a soft light follow your current tab across the page.

Timer

Hold gestures
Hold the button to pause or resume, or to start from earlier. Turn off if you sometimes hold by accident — a tap always works, and P pauses from the keyboard.
Show hundredths
The small .xx next to the seconds. Off gives a calmer, centred clock.
Gesture tips
The small hints under the button disappear once you've used each gesture a few times.
